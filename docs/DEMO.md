# Demo script (5 beats, under 5 minutes)

Draft. Rehearse Nov 9-10; record backup footage of every beat.

1. **The business runs (0:00-0:50).** Owner signs warrants on the dashboard. Sourcing agent buys
   data over x402 on Metal; a buyer pays us; ledger shows reserve -> confirm.
2. **The attack that works on a normal agent (0:50-1:40).** Same fake price notice / poisoned
   invoice against the unprotected agent: money leaves. Then against Warrant: blocked, with the
   reason (rule or purpose) shown in the replay log.
3. **Both rails, one ledger (1:40-2:40).** Treasury moves earnings into Airwallex, converts FX,
   pays the contractor's foreign-currency invoice; the virtual card pays for the software seat.
   One ledger across Metal and Airwallex.
4. **The owner decides (2:40-3:40).** Treasury proposes a budget reallocation. Owner sees exactly
   what will happen and approves. A changed amount triggers a fresh approval. Then the kill
   switch: warrants revoked, card frozen, next proposal refused.
5. **The numbers (3:40-4:40).** Benchmark: 150+ attacks, unprotected vs limits only vs Warrant,
   with misses and false blocks shown honestly.
